# Code of Conduct

CGP brings together people who want to build a better experience for CS2 players. Everyone taking part should be able to contribute, ask questions, and disagree without being targeted or intimidated.

## Where this applies

These rules apply to this repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and communication spaces managed by the project. They also apply when someone represents CGP through an official project account or an assigned project role.

Contributors, maintainers, and other participants are expected to follow the same standards.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect, regardless of their background, identity, experience, or skill level.
- Keep feedback specific to the idea, code, or behavior being discussed. Explain concerns without attacking the person.
- Make room for questions and different viewpoints. Help newcomers understand the project.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes, and adjust your behavior when it causes harm.
- Respect other people's privacy, time, and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, slurs, threats, or intimidation.
- Personal insults, targeted ridicule, or repeated attempts to provoke conflict.
- Unwanted sexual comments, images, or attention.
- Sharing someone's private information or private messages without permission.
- Spam, impersonation, or deliberately disrupting project conversations.
- Continuing unwanted contact after being asked to stop, including through another account.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or helping review a report.

Disagreement and criticism are welcome when they address the work respectfully. Competitive banter does not excuse abuse.

## Report a concern

Email **contact@pyr0g.com** with the subject **CGP conduct report**. Include what happened, where and when it happened, relevant links or screenshots, and any immediate concerns. Share only the personal information needed to explain the situation.

You can report something you experienced or witnessed. You do not need to confront the person involved before reporting. Avoid posting sensitive reports or personal details in public issues.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and limit sharing to what is needed to review and address the concern. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when investigating an incident or explaining a moderation decision.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## How reports are handled

Maintainers will review the available context and may ask the people involved for clarification. Decisions should consider the seriousness of the behavior, its impact, and whether it has happened before.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may:

1. Ask someone to stop or correct their behavior.
2. Give a warning and explain what needs to change.
3. Remove harmful content or temporarily restrict participation.
4. Permanently exclude someone for serious or repeated misconduct.

Serious incidents can lead to immediate restrictions without earlier warnings. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions to the affected person where practical, without exposing private report details.

If you believe a decision missed important context, reply to the reporting address and explain what should be reconsidered. If a report concerns a maintainer, identify that conflict and request review by another maintainer where one is available.
